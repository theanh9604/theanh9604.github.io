I’m delighted to share our recent research paper, presented at the 2025 15th International Conference on Power, Energy, and Electrical Engineering (CPEEE) in Fukuoka, Japan:
“Assessments of the Potential for Integrating Battery Energy Storage Systems into Vietnam National Grid”

🌎 This was my first international conference — a truly meaningful milestone! I had the opportunity to connect with brilliant professionals in the power systems field, and gain valuable insights that will stay with me for years to come.

---

In this study, we developed a Mixed Integer Linear Programming (MILP) model to solve the Unit Commitment problem for power plants integrated with Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

Our goal: identify cost-effective operating strategies that enhance grid reliability and improve investment returns for renewable energy projects in Vietnam.


I’d love to connect with researchers and professionals interested in energy optimization, renewables, storage, and power system planning.
